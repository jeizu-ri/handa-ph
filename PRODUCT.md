# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (PWA, mobile-first)

## Stack

Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, TanStack Query, MapLibre. Deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Filipinos checking typhoon and tropical cyclone wind signals (TCWS) for their home or current area, often on mid-range phones during active weather. Secondary: family members abroad checking on loved ones' areas.

## Product Purpose

Handa PH surfaces PAGASA-aligned wind signals, active cyclones, nationwide signal lists, and approximate storm tracks so people can prepare quickly. Success means the user sees their signal level within seconds and knows to verify with official PAGASA channels.

## Positioning

Unofficial mirror that reads public PAGASA bulletins directly (no API key required for core alerts), with clear disclaimers and one-tap links to DOST-PAGASA.

## Constraints

- Never present as official PAGASA/DOST.
- Life-safety decisions must defer to official channels.
- English UI first; Filipino labels where natural (e.g. "Aking signal").
- Must remain usable on slow networks (cached PWA shell).

## Voice

Calm, direct, authoritative without hype. Short sentences. No gamification.
